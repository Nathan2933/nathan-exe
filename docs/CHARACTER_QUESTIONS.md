# Character questions (one Google Form per friend)

Each real person in NATHAN.EXE gets a short form: 3 multiple-choice questions,
each with an **Other** option so they can write their own answer. Their answers
become that character's in-game conversation topics (they say it in their own
words, Nathan doesn't invent it).

Pop-culture characters (Tyler Durden, Arthur Morgan, Moon Knight, Spider-Man,
Daredevil, Richard Hendricks) are fictional, so they are not included.

**Form tip:** in Google Forms use *Multiple choice*, tick **Add "Other"**, and add a
last optional *Short answer*: "Anything you want your character to say in the
game?" Mark it optional. Add one line at the top: "Your answers will be shown in
Nathan's public portfolio game. Skip anything you'd rather not share."

## Turning answers into conversations

When the answers come back, send them (a CSV export from each Google Form is
fine). Each question becomes one dialogue topic for that character, in their
own words. For example:

> **Topic:** "What do you remember about school with Nathan?"
> **Line:** (their chosen answer, written as they'd say it)

Characters who appear on both islands (Guru, Anu, Shabesh, Thianesh, Akshai,
Naveen, Logeshwaran, Piranav) can reuse the same answers in both places, or fill
in a second form for Tahiti if they'd like different lines there.
